# Caching and query performance

## How do you stop dashboards and self-serve users overloading your database?

Embeddable protects your database in two layers. An in-memory cache is on by default and reduces repeated load on the database. Pre-aggregations compute and store summarised results in advance, so dashboards read from them instead of running heavy queries each time.

- [Level 1 cache: in-memory](/data-modeling/caching/in-memory): on by default; controlled with refresh keys.
- [Level 2 cache: pre-aggregations](/data-modeling/caching/level-2-cache/pre-aggregations): optional; the biggest gain for slow dashboards.

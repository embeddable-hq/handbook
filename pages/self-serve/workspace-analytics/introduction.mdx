# Workspace Analytics

<Callout emoji="⚠️">
  Workspace Analytics is in **Beta** and is rolling out to more workspaces over time. Expect more dashboards, filters, and detail as it matures. [Reach out to the Embeddable team](mailto:support@embeddable.com) if you have questions or run into issues.
</Callout>

**Workspace Analytics** gives you visibility into your own Embeddable dashboards. Instead of building and shipping dashboards blind, you can see how they're used, how they're performing, and where they're breaking — all from a set of ready-made dashboards inside your workspace.

<Callout emoji="💡">
  Workspace Analytics isn't a component you build with — it's a set of dashboards you use **alongside** the dashboards you've already built, similar to how you'd use the [Data Playground](/data-modeling/testing-models) alongside your data models.
</Callout>

## Why use Workspace Analytics?

As you roll out dashboards to your end-users, three questions come up quickly:

- **Is it broken?** Are any of my dashboards throwing errors right now, and for whom?
- **Is it used?** Are my end-users actually opening these dashboards, and how?
- **Is it fast?** Are queries loading quickly, or are some dashboards timing out or lagging?

Workspace Analytics answers all three, without any extra setup, using data Embeddable already collects on your behalf.

## Drill down or stay broad

Every Workspace Analytics dashboard has interactions and drill-downs built in.

- Start broad, to get a health check across your whole workspace.
- Click into any chart to drill down to a specific dashboard, query, or end-user.

This lets you move from "something feels off" to the exact cause, without leaving Embeddable.
